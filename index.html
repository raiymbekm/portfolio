About the project
Art director on NielsenIQ's global creative team for the past five years. I created the visual identity and marketing activation for the annual Breakthrough Innovation, Menu Innovation and Design Impact Awards, and led the look and feel of BASES, one of NIQ's biggest business units. As a senior specialist on the global creative and marketing team, I've overseen the company's design language and kept it consistent across most channels. Regional teams come to me for design guidance and ready-made assets such as social media templates. I've also project-managed many marketing campaigns and product launches, and I conceived, programmed and ran the Always-On campaigns, now a staple of every marketing team.

About the project
A retro, skeuomorphic identity in which each format borrows from its real-life counterpart: tape cassettes for the audio episodes, to signal a sonic experience, and VHS tapes for the YouTube thumbnails, to signal a visual one. I also made the short intro and opening sequence: the podcast logo as graffiti on streets and walls, for an international show about people from all walks of life.
